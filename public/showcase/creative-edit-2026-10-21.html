01 / STATIC CREATIVE

BATHER

A sale with
an editorial feel

The clothing stays at the center of the offer. Texture, warm light, and a close crop give the promotion a distinct visual identity.

THE HOOK
“End of Season Sale.”
THE OFFER
An extra 20% off select, already-reduced styles.
THE CREATIVE CHOICE
One photograph carries the mood; cream typography gives the offer room to breathe.

02 / STATIC CREATIVE

NATURAL DOG COMPANY

The bundle value
is the headline

A precise combined price anchors the composition, while oversized product packs make the two-item offer easy to understand.

THE HOOK
“Dinner-time care. $40.92 for both.”
THE OFFER
Buy Wild Alaskan Salmon Oil and get one Dental Powder tub 50% off.
THE CREATIVE CHOICE
The turquoise background separates the orange and blue packaging. The products become the main visual.

03 / STATIC CREATIVE

LEAF

One offer.
Two cyclist hooks.

The same gift-with-purchase proposition takes two forms: a direct value claim and a line built around cycling culture.

DIRECT VALUE
“Cyclists: Claim your $57 free gift today.”
AUDIENCE LANGUAGE
“No podium required to take home the gifts.”
SHARED OFFER
A free $57.90 gift set with the $109 Leaf Two. Both layouts show what is included.

04 / STATIC CREATIVE

FLAVCITY

The benefit fits
in the picture

The tote bag turns portability into something visible. The packet and shaker give the headline a concrete setting.

THE HOOK
“Your protein fits in your bag.”
THE OFFER
Subscribe and save 15%. The ad specifies 15 packets at $55.24 every four weeks.
THE CREATIVE CHOICE
Yellow and turquoise frame the vanilla product story, with the subscription details grouped at the bottom.

05 / STATIC CREATIVE

08 / MOTION CREATIVE

NEWPORT

A weight claim
you can see

The opening weight callout gives the film one clear idea. Footage of the motor being carried makes portability tangible.

OPENING CLAIM
“14.3 lbs motor weight.”
IN-USE DEMONSTRATION
